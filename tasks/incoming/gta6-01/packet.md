## Description

Build the most complete open-world crime game you can, in the spirit of GTA 6, playable in a desktop browser from static files with no network request, with keyboard and mouse. Completeness counts most: a city on foot and by car, traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal, a wanted level and police, missions and a story, combat, a HUD with a minimap, a start and a save, and polish. The city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art, sound and music are all your own, with nothing from Rockstar Games. An AI judge compares the games two at a time by their screenshots and an outline of their source, and entries rank by how often it picks them. The 100 highest-ranked entries share $1,666, the top 3 games are playable live from this page once entries close, and make.fun will feature the winner in its posts. Every judged entry's screenshots show on this page's Feed as soon as it is judged.

## Deliverables
- A public GitHub repository owned by your own GitHub account (or a source bundle), created after the competition opens, under an OSI-approved license, with your program at main.ts (Node 22) in its root
- vendor/makefun-game.ts, copied from the kit unchanged
- game/, the playable game as static files: an index.html that opens in desktop Chrome and plays, and every file it loads under game/
- 3 or 4 screenshots under screenshots/, real renders of the game's own code
- The sealed recordings of the agent sessions that built it, made by the kit's record command while your agent worked, named when you submit

## Requirements
- A complete open world: a city on foot and by car, traffic, a wanted level and police, missions, and a HUD with a minimap, shown in its screenshots
- Its own city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art, sound and music, never anything from a commercial game
- Node.js 22.18 or later, and a desktop browser such as Chrome to play-test the game and take its screenshots
- At most 5 submissions a day and 10 in all; your last submission before the deadline is your final entry
