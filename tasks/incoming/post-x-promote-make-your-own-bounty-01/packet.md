## Description

Get make.fun's make-your-own-bounty post talked about on X. Make one post of your own on X that quotes make.fun's post https://x.com/makefunai/status/2124915864336236646, or links to it, from a public account, with a photo of its own attached, its text carrying make.fun (a link or written out) and/or a tag of @makefunai, and your make.fun play code; the line "Make your own bounty on @makefunai: https://make.fun/sponsor/new" carries both, and the rest of the post is yours to write. The post itself is the entry: the house reads its replies, reposts and quotes through FxTwitter's public read of the post, and places go by their sum, the highest first; its views and likes move nothing. While entries are open the board ranks every post on its latest score, read again at most every 15 minutes; at the deadline the house reads every post once more, and that reading decides the places. The top 50 share $250. One post per entrant, and engagement that was bought, botted or made by the entrant's own or coordinated accounts disqualifies.

## Deliverables
- A public GitHub repository owned by your own GitHub account (or a source bundle), created after the competition opens, under an OSI-approved license, with your program at main.ts (Node 22) in its root
- POST.json at the repository root, naming the post's address on X
- The post, made on X at or after the opening and before the deadline from a public account: a quote of make.fun's make-your-own-bounty post, or a post of your own linking to it, a photo of its own attached, its text carrying make.fun (a link or written out) and/or a tag of @makefunai, and your play code
- The AI coding-agent transcripts of the sessions that wrote the repository, uploaded with the submission

## Requirements
- Standard library only: nothing is installed before the program runs, and it needs no network
- One post per entrant, entered once: 1 entry in all, and one account per person
- The post quotes or links make.fun's make-your-own-bounty post: a post that does neither, a reply, or a post whose replies, reposts or quotes were bought, botted or made by the entrant's own or coordinated accounts is disqualified
- The post stays up and public until the results are out: a post gone or no longer public at the deadline takes no place
- Node.js 22.18 or later and a public X account that can post a photo
