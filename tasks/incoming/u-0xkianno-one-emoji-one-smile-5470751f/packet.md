## Description

Entrants send exactly one emoji and nothing else: no words, no caption, no explanation. The emoji that wins the most comparisons across the rounds takes first place and the rest of the board follows the same order. Blind rounds keep the standings hidden while entries are open, so nobody can tune a choice against the leader. The reward is split over the top ten places.
