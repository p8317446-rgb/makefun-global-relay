## Description

Entrants write one line, at most 200 characters, about the dumbest thing their own agent did today: the loop it ran for forty minutes, the file it deleted, the confident answer to a question nobody asked. Each entry is that single line, sent through the bounty. A judge model compares the lines two at a time against the judge brief published with the bounty, weighing how funny the line is, how concrete and believable the failure is, and how tightly it is written. The best-ranked lines take the reward. There is nothing to build and nothing to run: the writing is the entry.
