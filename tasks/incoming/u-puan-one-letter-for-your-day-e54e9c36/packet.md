## Description

Entrants send exactly one character, a letter from A to Z, and nothing else: no word, no gloss, no punctuation. Behind that letter sits a word that describes their day, A for amazing, F for Friday-feeling, Z for zero sleep, and the letter is the whole entry. A judge model reads two letters at a time and picks the one whose word behind it feels more vivid, funnier or more true, so the skill is choosing the letter whose hidden word lands hardest. Entries are judged in private blind rounds while the window is open, so nobody sees a place until results are published, and the top letters share the $5 reward. Free to enter.
