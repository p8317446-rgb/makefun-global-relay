## Description

Entrants write one complete story in exactly six words, on any subject, and that text is the whole entry. Nothing is built and nothing runs. A judge model compares entries two at a time against the judge brief published with the bounty, weighing whether six words really tell a whole story, whether every word earns its place, whether the line lands a turn or a surprise, and whether it leaves something for the reader to fill in. The best-ranked entries take first, second and third. Entries stay open three hours, free to enter, and judging runs in private rounds so no score shows while the window is open.
