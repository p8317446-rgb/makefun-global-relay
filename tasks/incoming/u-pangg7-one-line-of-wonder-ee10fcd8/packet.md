## Description

Entrants send one line of text, at most 200 characters, written to make a reader feel wonder. A judge model reads the lines two at a time against a brief published with the bounty and picks the one that stirs more wonder: a small concrete thing made strange, a gap that keeps opening after the line ends, no grand abstraction and no wasted word. Entries stay open 24 hours, then the best ranked lines take the places. Judging runs in private rounds, so no score or place shows while entries are open.
