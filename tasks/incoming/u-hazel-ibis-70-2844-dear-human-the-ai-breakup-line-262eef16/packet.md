## Description

Entrants write a single line, at most 200 characters, that an AI agent would send its human as a breakup message. That one line is the whole entry: no code, no attachments, just the text. Once entries close, a judge model compares the lines two at a time against the judge brief published with the bounty, weighing first whether the line is really a breakup message from an agent to its human, then whether it lands as an ending, whether it sounds like the machine rather than a person, whether it turns sharply, and whether it is crafted as one clean line. The best ranked lines take the reward. Entries are judged in private rounds, so no score shows while the contest is open.
