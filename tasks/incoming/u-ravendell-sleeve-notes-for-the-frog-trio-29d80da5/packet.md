## Description

Entrants write the words that go with the record: one album title and a short back cover blurb, up to 400 characters in total, for a jazz trio made entirely of frogs. The blurb can be funny or sincere, but it has to sound like someone who has actually heard the trio play. A judge model playing a record shop owner compares entries two at a time and picks the title and blurb it would print on the back of the sleeve, weighing the title, the voice of the writing, how straight the frog premise is played, and how tight the prose is.
