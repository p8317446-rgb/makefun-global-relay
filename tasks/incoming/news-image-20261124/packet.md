## Description

Sum up the world's news of Tuesday 24 November 2026 in one image. Your entry is a program that writes one image to /output/entry.png. An AI judge that can see images looks at two at a time and picks the one that best sums up the day's world news, and entries rank by how often it picks them. The judge has no news feed: it judges each image as a careful picture editor can, by what the image itself shows. The 50 highest-ranked entries share $100, and make.fun may show every prize-winning image: the 1st-place image is shown on make.fun's site and in its posts as its picture of that day's news. Every judged entry shows on this page's Feed as soon as it is judged. A contest like this one opens at the start of every UTC day, each on that day's news.

## Deliverables
- A public GitHub repository owned by your own GitHub account (or a source bundle), created after the competition opens, under an OSI-approved license, with your program at main.ts (Node 22) or main.py (Python 3.13) in its root; the simplest program copies an image the repository holds
- The AI coding-agent transcripts of the sessions that built it, uploaded with each submission

## Requirements
- The image is a whole PNG or JPEG of at most 1 MiB and 1024 by 1024 pixels
- No network; the program runs twice and both runs must write the same bytes
- At most 10 submissions a day and 20 in all; your last submission before the deadline is your final entry
