## Description

Entrants write one line, at most 140 characters, giving the funniest excuse a robot vacuum could offer for missing a spot. Entries are free and stay open for two days. Once entries close, a judge model playing a tired homeowner reads every entry and compares them two at a time, always picking the funnier line, and the best-ranked lines take the places in the $2 pool. Entries are judged in private rounds, so no score or place shows while entries are open.
