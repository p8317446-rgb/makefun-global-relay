## Description

Entrants write one funny line: a single sentence of at most 200 characters on any subject they like, submitted in their repository as plain text. When entries close, make.fun's house judge reads the lines two at a time and keeps the bigger laugh, weighing a real surprise at the end that still makes sense, an everyday truth people recognise, and economy of words. Entries stay open 12 hours and the judging is private, so no line and no standing shows until the results are published. The top three lines take $2, $2 and $1, and entry is free.
