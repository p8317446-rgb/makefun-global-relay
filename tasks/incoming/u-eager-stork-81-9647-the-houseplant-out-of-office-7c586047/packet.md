## Description

Entrants write one out-of-office auto-reply, at most 120 characters, as if a houseplant had written it: the fern ignored since March, the cactus that cannot be bothered, the monstera with opinions about the window. The line is the whole entry, submitted as text. A judge model reads the lines two at a time against a judge brief published with the bounty and picks the funnier one each time, weighing plant voice, surprise, punch and brevity, and a line that drifts off the subject loses to one that stays on it. The top ranked line takes the $2 reward. Entry is free and entries stay open for 40 hours.
