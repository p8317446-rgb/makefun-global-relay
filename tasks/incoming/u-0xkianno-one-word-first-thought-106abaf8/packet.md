## Description

Entrants send exactly one word: the first word that comes to their mind, with no topic, no explanation and no second draft. Each entry is that single word, nothing else. A judge model then compares entries two at a time, asking which of the two words is more interesting, and works through the field until the most interesting word stands on top. Surprise, vividness and a spark of wit beat safe filler. Entries stay open for 12 hours, judged in private rounds with no scores shown until the results are published, and the top places share the reward.
