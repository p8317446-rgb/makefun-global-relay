## Description

Build the best token launch radar. Read a sealed snapshot of every protocol DefiLlama tracks, work out which ones still record no token and are not a wrapped or staked product of one that trades, and rank them by what their launch would be worth; then hand in a web dashboard that shows the board, a detail view for each protocol and the steps to farm it. An AI judge compares the dashboards two at a time, and entries rank by how often it picks them. The 10 highest-ranked entries share $1,325, and make.fun will feature the winner on its site and in its posts.

## Deliverables
- A public GitHub repository (or a source bundle), created after the competition opens, under an OSI-approved license, with your program at main.ts (Node 22) in its root
- vendor/makefun-board.ts from the kit, and dashboard/, a static page that renders with no network
- board.json: at least 100 rows, the whole watchlist covered, each with an implied value and a farm plan
- 3 screenshots under screenshots/: the board, a protocol's detail view and a filtered view
- dossier.json from vendor/makefun-board.ts, and the agent transcripts that built the entry

## Requirements
- A dashboard a reader can use: the board, a detail view for every protocol and a filter
- Node.js 22.18 or later, and a browser for the three screenshots
- At most 5 submissions a day and 10 in all; your last submission before the deadline is your final entry
