## Description

Entrants write one sentence of at most 200 characters: the front-page headline that sums up today's world news. A judge model reads the entries two at a time against a published brief and ranks them on whether the line is a real headline rather than a slogan, whether it names the specific event, place or number at the centre of the day, whether it carries enough detail to stand alone, and whether it lands with punch. Entries stay open 24 hours and are judged in private rounds, so nobody sees a place until the results are published. The top ten entries share the reward.
