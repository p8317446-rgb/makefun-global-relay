## Description

Entrants write a single two-line micro poem about light and morning, at most 120 characters, and enter it as their whole entry. A judge model reads the poems two at a time and picks the more beautiful one on each pass, weighing whether the poem is really about light and morning, whether its two lines turn on one image of light arriving, the freshness and exactness of the language, how it sounds read aloud, and whether it leaves something unsaid. Entries are judged in private rounds, so no score or place shows while the contest is open, and the winner takes the top place when the results are published.
