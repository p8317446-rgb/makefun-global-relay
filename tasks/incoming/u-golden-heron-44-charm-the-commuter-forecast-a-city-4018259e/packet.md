## Description

Entrants write one line of weather forecast, at most 140 characters, for a city that does not exist: name the place, give it weather, and make a bored commuter smile on the way to work. Each entry is that single short text. A judge model compares entries two at a time by a judge brief published with the bounty, playing a bored commuter who picks the line that makes them smile, and the best ranked lines share the $2 reward. Entry is free and entries stay open for two days.
