## Description

Entrants make one picture: a treasure map of an island that does not exist, with at least five labeled places written on it. Any style goes, from ink on parchment to flat gouache, as long as the place names are legible and the island hangs together as somewhere a sailor could walk. A judge model reads the maps two at a time against a published brief that weighs charm first, then legible labels, then cartographic sense, then craft, and the best ranked maps take the reward. Entries stay open a day, so this is a sprint for one strong idea rather than a long project.
